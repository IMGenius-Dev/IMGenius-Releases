THE DETAILS

What private and offline mean in IMGenius

Local conversation inference

Character-chat responses are generated by your locally installed Ollama model, not a developer-operated cloud chat server. An internet connection is not required for local inference after installation.

Files on your computer

IMGenius stores app data such as chat databases and character information in your operating system's IMGenius user-data directory. Ollama manages its own model files; optional ComfyUI images and models are saved locally according to your setup.

What we do not automatically collect

The current local-chat workflow does not include automatic uploads of chat text, character profiles, generated images, or a built-in developer analytics service. We don't need your conversation content for the local AI to respond.

When IMGenius uses the internet

IMGenius checks the public GitHub Releases feed for updates; that request contacts GitHub. Model discovery, downloading models and installers, and any optional remote services you configure also communicate with their respective providers. These online functions are not required to run an already-installed local chat.

Support reports are your choice

If you explicitly submit a report to an operator-configured support relay, the issue title, description, optional saved username, and images you attach are sent to that relay. Reports are user-initiated, and this feature may not be configured in every beta build.

Choose local services for privacy

For fully local chat, run Ollama on your machine; optional local ComfyUI runs there too. Connecting a remote service or visiting third-party model sites is different from local-only operation and is subject to the privacy behavior of those services.

Bottom line: Download your tools and models once, then disconnect if you want. IMGenius local character chat works without internet access and does not automatically send your personal chats to us. Online downloads, GitHub update checks, and explicitly submitted support reports are separate network features.
