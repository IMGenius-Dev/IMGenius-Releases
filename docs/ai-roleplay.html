STORYTELLING YOUR WAY

What is an AI roleplay chatbot?

AI roleplay chat is a creative conversation where a language model responds in the voice of a fictional character. You set the scene and decide what to say or do; the model generates dialogue and narrative responses based on the character's setup and the current conversation.

IMGenius supports character-based chats using local language models through Ollama. The result can be a quiet conversation, a mystery, a science-fiction scene, or a longer fictional adventure. The quality and consistency of responses depend on the chosen model and the character's details.

Three ways to start a scene

A mysterious encounter

A stranger knows something about a long-forgotten place. Start with a question and let the conversation reveal the setting.

Everyday character dialogue

Build a persona with a distinctive point of view and chat casually. Explore their opinions, habits, and fictional backstory.

An ongoing adventure

Create a character with a goal and a scenario with stakes. Bring up earlier events to help the model maintain the thread of the story.

How to make AI roleplay feel more natural

Set a clear character personality and location before you begin. Mention details that matter to the scene, keep your actions understandable, and use the editor to adjust the character when the model drifts. If a conversation takes an unwanted turn, you can edit, continue, or regenerate messages using the available chat controls.

For longer stories, different models can maintain context differently. Save key facts in your character description or scenario when they should stay consistent; don't assume a model will remember an unlimited history.

Private, local-first roleplay

With IMGenius, the local Ollama model handles character responses on your device. Conversations and character cards are stored locally rather than automatically uploaded to an IMGenius chat service. Once dependencies and your model are installed, you can use local character chat without an internet connection.

Creative themes and content boundaries depend on the model you install, its configuration, and applicable terms and laws; IMGenius does not guarantee a particular output. Online features such as update checks, downloads, and optional support reports are described on the privacy page.
