BUILD YOUR CAST

What makes a memorable AI character?

A name and a greeting are a start, but a memorable character needs details that shape how they speak and react. Describe what motivates them, how they treat others, and what they know about their fictional world. A shorter, specific personality often works better than a long list of vague adjectives.

Start with a character card

In the IMGenius character editor, you can work with descriptions, personality details, scenarios, greetings, and other character-card fields. Compatible Character Card V2/V3 data can be imported; content available from any given file depends on its format and metadata.

EXAMPLE CHARACTER OUTLINE
Name
Mira — night-shift radio operator
Personality
Curious, skeptical, quick to joke; protective of her crew
Scenario
A storm has isolated a research station; the last radio signal is unexplained
First message
“You picked a strange night to call. Tell me you're not out there in this weather.”
Dialogue notes
Short, vivid dialogue; notices background sounds; asks pointed questions

Refine the way they speak

Give your character a consistent voice. Consider whether they speak formally or casually, whether they use humor, and what they avoid discussing. Add a clear scenario so conversations begin with context instead of a generic greeting. Use alternate first messages when you want different starting points for the same character.

Use local AI to help draft a character

IMGenius v0.7.9 includes an AI-assisted character drafting feature. Supply a character name and brief description, then use the selected local Ollama model to generate draft fields. Review the output before saving: your edits are what make the character your own.

Explore your character through roleplay

Once the card is ready, start a chat and test how the character responds to the kind of situations you imagined. Adjust the description, personality, or opening message when the dialogue feels off. Local conversations give you room to experiment without requiring developer-hosted character chats.
